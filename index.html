About

I have the hands of an enabler. Give me a space to organize, a team to hop into, or a problem to crack, I'll spot the essentials and give a little push to function.

I've surfed between design, content creation, and project coordination — exploring different territories on purpose to see how the pieces fit together and what actually deserves attention. Currently, I'm digging into software development to understand the craft and build my own projects along the way.

I would love to be the connector between creative minds, technical teams, and the customer. I'm at my best in the middle of projects where everyone needs to be heard and everything needs to click.

My site is still evolving and my gallery is on the way, but you can get to know me a little better on LinkedIn. Feel free to suggest a project or just say hi!
