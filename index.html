Visuals

My style of working is very sensitive and intuitive. Step 1 begins when I look for the essentials and visualise the big picture in my mind. I then explore options and test ideas through mind-mapping, building a few solid suggestions to present to the client and colleagues for feedback. Step 2 is a phase where the real implementation happens. This step is the most delicious part of the process, and a phase where the client often gets the final light-bulb moment. When the creative solution or direction is decided, my doing-engine gears up and delivers the final product with thoughtful details.
